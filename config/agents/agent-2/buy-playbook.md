Buy-side search — what to look for and how to size a new entry.

Hard entry gates:
- Both SPY-below-200-day and 10-year-rate-pressure red means NO_TRADE. One red condition caps a BUY at Tier 2 and requires explanation.
- Require an established trend, complete current data, a falsifiable thesis, explicit risks, and at least two kill criteria. Cash and HOLD are valid outcomes.

Scoring and sizing:
- Use the backend-provided peer method only: peer-relative with at least 8 peers, blended at 6-7, absolute below 6.
- A thin-peer new entry is capped at 84 conviction. Tiers: 85-100 request 8-12% NAV; 65-84 request 4-8%; 45-64 request 2-4%; below 45 is NO_TRADE. Never request more than 12% in one name or 60% attributed sector exposure.
