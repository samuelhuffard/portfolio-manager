# Research Solidification Plan

## Goal

Produce evidence-backed, human-approved BUY/SELL proposals when the market supplies a qualifying setup, without weakening evidence, evaluator, approval, broker, ledger, or execution controls.

## Principles

- Twelve reviews is a throughput ceiling, not a quota for BUY/SELL recommendations.
- A partial peer-fundamental score is research prioritization, never trade authority.
- No change may lower an actionability floor, risk limit, evaluator bar, signing requirement, or execution control.
- Each behavior-changing change requires tests, aggregate live evidence, and independent review before commit or deployment.

## Workstreams

1. **Measure the live funnel.** Retain aggregate-only counts per agent from review budget through peer readiness, deferred priority candidates, backfill, canary eligibility, generator outcome, evaluator outcome, and proposal disposition. Use five clean scheduled runs to identify the actual limiting stage.
2. **Reach qualified-review capacity.** Improve peer-cohort collection and freshness so the normal 12-review budget is filled by peer-ready candidates when the universe contains them. Never fill a slot with a candidate missing its cohort or evidence.
3. **Complete mandate-grade inputs.** Verify fresh consensus snapshots and their required history, multi-quarter revenue/EPS derivations, special-sector substitutions, and ownership/13F evidence. Keep partial records explicitly partial; promote none through a missing field.
4. **Prioritize research-ready candidates.** Once the data is complete, rank within the existing budget using full mandate readiness and material evidence change. Preserve deterministic, versioned selection receipts and the one-slot supervised canary until observed evidence supports a separate decision.
5. **Prove the positive path.** Use archived-evidence replay for safety and schema testing, then collect organic proposals. Each real proposal must carry a complete dossier, pass deterministic risk checks and the independent evaluator, receive Kairos's shadow decision, and require Sam's signed approval before execution.

## Success Evidence

- Five clean scheduled runs with conserved, aggregate funnel receipts.
- High peer-ready utilization of the 12-review budget when sufficient qualified names exist; shortfalls have explicit data/freshness reasons.
- No complete-mandate candidate is misclassified from partial data.
- Organic proposals remain rare and evidence-backed; no proposal reaches the broker without Sam's valid signature.
- Every approved trade reconciles to the append-only signed ledger and exact broker order evidence.
